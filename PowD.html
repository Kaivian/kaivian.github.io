“Electronic music is not just sound—it is physical pressure, room tension, and shared frequency.”

— POWD • Studio Philosophy & Live Performance

PowD is an electronic music producer, DJ, and sonic architect crafting dark, driving, and bass-heavy sets. Emerging from the club circuit, his sound blends analog warmth, punchy low-end pressure, and versatile open format groove architecture.

Driven by the energy of late-night warehouse and club sessions, PowD's sets are engineered to create physical tension and collective euphoria. Every transition is an intentional narrative across the full 120–174 BPM spectrum.

With a distinct visual identity characterized by espresso-toned minimalism, high-fashion silhouettes, and architectural restraint, PowD treats both music and aesthetic as a singular unified discipline.
